## The word

**AIYOH**

The single word a grandma would text about AI: the warm, baffled
exclamation a Southeast Asian grandma types when her grandchild shows her
a talking robot. It carries the AI right in its first two letters, and
the rest is pure grandma — a little startled, a little delighted, and
instantly readable.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the word to `/output/entry.txt` — one word, nothing else,
exactly as the bounty asks.
